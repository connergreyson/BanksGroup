Georgetown

Marty and Tracy live in Georgetown. They are the neighbors who also happen to be your agents—on the Square on a weeknight, at the Red Poppy Festival in the spring, and in living rooms all over town. They help buyers and sellers across Georgetown and the surrounding communities, not only in the names that show up on a “top neighborhoods” list.

What Georgetown feels like

Georgetown is Williamson County’s seat, a college town, and a place people still walk downtown. If you are relocating, this is the texture of daily life—not a brochure version of it.

The Square

The historic courthouse square is the heart of town—often called one of the most beautiful town squares in Texas. Independent shops, patios, galleries, and Saturday mornings that actually get used. If you want to understand Georgetown, start here.

Restaurants & nights out

Locals cycle through Wildfire on the Square, Monument Cafe, City Post Chophouse, Roots, and Red Horn Coffee House & Taproom. Date night, tacos, live music, and coffee that turns into an evening—without driving into Austin.

Festivals & traditions

The Red Poppy Festival takes over downtown every spring—the city’s official flower, a parade, music, and the whole town out. In December, the Lighting of the Square turns the courthouse into a holiday landmark. These are the weekends people move here for.

Outdoors & everyday spots

Blue Hole and the San Gabriel River trails, Inner Space Cavern, Southwestern University, and shopping at Wolf Ranch Town Center. Lake Georgetown is close. Surrounding towns—Liberty Hill, Round Rock, Leander—sit in the same orbit.

Neighborhoods

These are the communities buyers ask for most. Marty and Tracy work all of them—and the streets that never make a list. New construction, established golf-course neighborhoods, 55+ in Sun City, historic homes near the Square, and pocket communities in between. If it is Georgetown, they know it.
